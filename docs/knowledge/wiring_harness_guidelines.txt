Wiring Harness Routing Guidelines

Wiring harness routing changes should verify:

- minimum clearance from adjacent components
- bend radius
- connector strain relief
- abrasion and chafing risk
- clamp and support locations
- thermal exposure
- vibration movement
- electrical connector integrity

Routing changes should be validated under expected vibration and thermal conditions.

Changes affecting harness routing near structural or moving components should receive mechanical and electrical review.

Updated routing should be documented before release.
